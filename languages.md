# The Language Diaries
![Language learning and books](images/languages.jpg)

*Every language opens a new door.*

## Why Learn Another Language?

Learning a language is about more than vocabulary. It is about **communication**, *curiosity* and ***connecting with people***.

> Language learning is a journey.
>
> Every new expression brings us closer to understanding another culture.

### Three Ways to Practise

1. Listen to music or podcasts.
2. Read short articles.
   1. Highlight unfamiliar words.
   2. Look up their meanings.
3. Practise speaking regularly.

## French Expressions Worth Knowing

| French | English meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Ça marche ! | That works! |
| Bon courage ! | Good luck / Hang in there! |
| Ça me dit. | I'm up for it. |

The French expression `Ça marche !` is useful in everyday conversation.

### A Tiny Language Exercise

```text
Bonjour !
Hello!

Comment ça va ?
How are you?
```
