# Culture Without Borders

*Different traditions. Shared humanity.*

## Exploring Cultural Connections

Culture shapes how we communicate, celebrate, eat and understand the world.

### Food, Language and Identity

Food tells stories about history, family and belonging. A dish can connect generations and preserve memories.

#### Cultural Traditions

Different communities celebrate important moments in unique ways.

##### Small Details, Big Meanings

Greetings, gestures and expressions can carry meanings that are not immediately obvious to visitors.

###### A Final Thought

Curiosity and respect are essential when discovering unfamiliar traditions.

## Words Across Cultures

Consider these expressions:

- **Bon appétit** — a French expression used before eating.
- **Sannu** — a Hausa greeting.
- **Welcome** — an English greeting of hospitality.
